# Final player layout captures

These are the latest R1 device captures, refreshed on October 3 during the
layout clipping audit. Both Now Playing and lyrics were captured for all seven
layouts. Default, Gallery, Orbit, Panorama, Vinyl, Hiby’s and Hiby’s Graph
have been reviewed and approved.
Gallery and Orbit play/pause rings were checked after correcting LVGL image
bounds and sampling; layouts use their normal image sizing.
The matching playback and lyrics views use
**The Silent Man — Dream Theater** from the SD card, with its existing synced lyrics.
