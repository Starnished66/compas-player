Selector logo sources
Runtime: embedded C arrays in src/bootloader/selector_logos*.c, generated
by scripts/generate_selector_logos.py. Each bootloader links only the
width matching BOARD_SCREEN_WIDTH. The standalone R3II 2025 loader also
omits the Rockbox arrays.

Verified with ImageMagick 7.1.2-32 (magick).

Compás wordmark
  Source: assets/brand/compas-wordmark.svg (URW Gothic Book outlines,
  same path as src/ui/brand_marks.c).
  Embedded as A8 (alpha only), tinted at draw time with the selector
  title colour.

Rockbox logo
  File: assets/bootloader/selector/rockbox-logo.svg
  Official artwork from the Rockbox project, not redrawn.
  Copyright: the Rockbox project.
  Source URL:
    https://git.rockbox.org/cgit/rockbox.git/plain/docs/logo/rockbox-logo.svg
  License: GNU GPL version 2 or (at your option) any later version, as
  shipped in Rockbox docs/COPYING:
    https://git.rockbox.org/cgit/rockbox.git/plain/docs/COPYING
  GitHub mirror of the same files:
    https://github.com/Rockbox/rockbox/tree/master/docs/logo
  The SVG includes a full-canvas black backing rect. The generator
  crops the gold plate and scales it to the selector row heights
  (50px on 480-wide panels, 34px on 320-wide).
