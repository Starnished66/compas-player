Breeze top-bar status icons

Source: https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons (tag v6.31.0)
License: LGPL-3.0-or-later (see BREEZE_COPYING-ICONS.txt)
Copyright (C) 2014 Uri Herrera <uri_herrera@nitrux.in> and others

Rendered from the 22px symbolic SVGs straight to 28x28 RGBA PNG (122 DPI);
the battery icons are 35x35 (153 DPI) so their body matches the others
with ImageMagick. The Breeze text colour is replaced with white so the
app can recolour the glyphs.
