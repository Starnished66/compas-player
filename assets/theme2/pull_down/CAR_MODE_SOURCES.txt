Car Mode quick-toggle icons

car_mode.png and car_mode_s.png are original Compas Player artwork generated by
scripts/generate_car_mode_toggle.py with Pillow drawing primitives. The artwork
uses the pull-down toggle's 84 x 84 px circular format and its existing off/on
palette. The car symbol was drawn from geometric shapes for this project; no
third-party or stock-device artwork was copied or modified.

Regenerate from the repository root with:
  python3 scripts/generate_car_mode_toggle.py
